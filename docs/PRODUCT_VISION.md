# Product Vision – CH EdTech

**Product Name:** CH EdTech  
**Tagline:** See Mathematics. Understand Geometry. Learn Smarter.  
**Core Purpose:** To empower teachers and high school students to truly comprehend spatial mathematics and solid geometry through rigorous interactive 3D visualizations, automated geometric constraint solving, and pedagogical AI assistance.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Mathematical Truth Over Screen Projections:** The geometry engine computes spatial relations using exact vector math, linear algebra, and coordinate transformations rather than visual approximation.
2. **Pedagogical AI Agent:** Instead of simply spitting out text solutions, the AI agent ingests problem statements (via text or OCR), identifies geometric entities, constructs the 3D model step-by-step, and verifies mathematical consistency.
3. **Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure:** Decoupled Object Storage, pgvector semantic search, full telemetry observability, and multi-tier security defenses.
